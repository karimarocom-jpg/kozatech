About Koza

Koza is a limited liability company (Besloten Vennootschap) registered in Belgium since 2007 and active in the construction sector. We value quality workmanship, clear communication and respect for our clients' time and privacy.

Today we also develop and publish mobile applications, applying the same principles: simple, dependable and transparent about data.
